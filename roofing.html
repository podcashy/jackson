Roofing Installation

A strong, secure roof is the ultimate line of defense for any structure. At Jack Interiors & Fittings, we offer precision roofing installation services using top-grade structural timber, stone-coated steel tiles, and heavy-duty iron sheets. Our expert team ensures correct pitch alignment, sturdy framing, and seamless waterproofing to give your residential or commercial property enduring structural integrity and a majestic finish.

Roofing Insulation

Proper insulation is crucial for maintaining comfortable indoor temperatures throughout the changing seasons. We install high-performance thermal and acoustic insulation materials underneath your roofing sheets. This technology effectively blocks outdoor heat from baking your interiors during hot days, traps warmth during chilly nights, and significantly muffles the sound of heavy rainfall, ensuring a peaceful and energy-efficient living environment.
